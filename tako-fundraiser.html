Our Story

Born from Love & Loss

TAKO was co-founded by Shivay, then just 14 years old, and his father Anirban after a heartbreaking experience — Shivay's pet parrot passed away while the family was away because a shelter failed to provide proper care.

Determined to make sure no family had to go through that pain again, they built TAKO — the Trusted Animal Kare Organization — right here in Westford, MA. Today TAKO works with local NGOs, veterinarians, and activists to rescue pets from hunger, abuse, and helplessness.

  • 🏥
    Animal Medical Care — Connecting rescued animals with veterinary treatment and rehabilitation.
  • 🏠
    Pet Rescue & Adoption — Placing injured and homeless animals into loving forever homes.
  • 🤝
    Community Partnerships — Working with local NGOs and activists across the region.
  • 📚
    Education & Outreach — Teaching responsible pet ownership and animal welfare in schools.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? Email us at info@takofoundation.org

Can I walk without a dog?
+
Absolutely! You don't need a dog to participate. All animal lovers, families, and supporters are welcome. Many participants walk in honor of a pet they've lost or as a general show of support.
Is the $20 fee per person or per dog?
+
The $20 registration fee is per human participant. Your dogs walk for free! If you're bringing multiple human participants (e.g., family members), each person needs their own registration.
Can I register on the day of the event?
+
Yes! Day-of registration is welcome starting at 8:00 AM at the registration booth. However, t-shirts and swag bags are only guaranteed for those who register online before June 14. The registration fee is the same ($20).
What should I bring?
+
Comfortable walking shoes, your dog's leash, a poop bag (extras provided on-site), water for you and your pup, sunscreen, and your good vibes! We'll have water stations along the route.
Are there age restrictions?
+
None at all — this is a family-friendly event! Children under 12 walk free when accompanied by a registered adult. Strollers and wheelchairs are welcome on the paved route.
Where does the money go?
+
100% of registration proceeds go directly to TAKO Foundation programs including animal rescue, veterinary medical care, and adoption services. TAKO is a volunteer-run organization — no administrative overhead from your registration fee.
Can I volunteer instead of (or in addition to) registering?
+
Yes! We'd love volunteer help. Email info@takofoundation.org with the subject "Volunteer — June 21" and we'll send you details. Volunteers are encouraged to register for the walk too!
